🌱 Daymark Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

Daymark ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Daymark mobile application.

Information We Collect

Daymark allows users to create and manage habits, reminders, streaks, and progress information. Most data is stored locally on your device.

The application stores habit information, settings, and progress data locally on your device using local storage technologies.

Personal Information

Daymark does not require user registration and does not intentionally collect personal identification information through its core functionality.

Notifications

Daymark may request notification permission to provide habit reminders and scheduled alerts.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share user data with third parties.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user data, but no storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Daymark does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page.
